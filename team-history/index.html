Notch Run Club
Hall of Fame

Runners never retire. We just move into higher age groups. Baseball makes you wait until you have been retired for five years before you are eligible. At Notch, you have to have run with us for at least five USATF seasons and receive 75% of the vote from eligible members of the Hall of Fame Committee.

Inducted

2025

Kat Vassallo

A former standout at Colby College, Kat was our first ever inductee. She was one of the founding members of our women’s team, and her win at the Cape Cod Marathon, the first win for any Notch athlete at a USATF race, sent a signal that the team had grown into a true contender.

Inducted

2026

Brett Ely

Brett was quoted in a 2012 article saying she “thought her competitive running days were behind her” when she started a PhD program. That was just before she qualified for the Olympic Trials for the fourth time. A full 13 years after announcing the end of her competitive career, she was the national indoor age group champion in the 3K. She then won two more national titles outdoors. She has represented the US at the Pan Am Games, and she served as a women’s captain for Notch RC in 2023.
